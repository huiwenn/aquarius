## Paper & Description Insights
XFID focuses on three representative Xinjiang Uyghur folk instruments: Dutar (都塔尔), Satar (萨塔尔), Tanbur (弹布尔). Contains approximately 4 hours of lossless audio recordings with aligned MIDI annotations and readable sheet music.

This dataset is significant for representing Xinjiang Uyghur minority musical traditions — an underrepresented area in Chinese MIR. The inclusion of aligned MIDI and sheet music alongside audio makes it multimodal. The three instruments are all plucked/bowed string instruments from the Uyghur tradition.

## Content & Taxonomy Analysis
- Time period: Contemporary recordings of traditional Xinjiang folk music
- Region: Xinjiang (新疆), Uyghur ethnic minority
- Genre/form: Xinjiang folk instrumental music
- Instrumentation: Dutar (plucked lute), Satar (bowed lute), Tanbur (plucked lute) — Central Asian string instrument family
- Musical system: Uyghur musical modes (maqam tradition, distinct from Han pentatonic)
- Modalities: Audio (lossless), MIDI (aligned), sheet music
- Labels: instrument identity, MIDI note events
- Notable: One of few datasets covering non-Han Chinese minority music traditions; multimodal (audio+MIDI+scores)
