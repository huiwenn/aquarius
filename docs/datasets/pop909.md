## Paper & Description Insights
POP909 is a dataset of 909 Chinese pop songs designed for music arrangement generation research. Each song has MIDI files with separated arrangement tracks (melody, sub-melody, accompaniment), beat timing information (from both audio and MIDI), chord annotations with timestamps, and key change information. Pre-processed versions in Magenta event token format are also available.

The dataset is notable for its scale (909 songs) and the quality of arrangement-level annotations, making it valuable for studying melody-accompaniment relationships in Chinese pop music. Related tools include Google Magenta's Music Transformer and Performance RNN.

## Content & Taxonomy Analysis
- Time period: Contemporary Chinese pop
- Region: Mainland China / pan-Chinese pop
- Genre/form: C-pop / Mandopop
- Instrumentation: Not explicitly labeled per instrument; arrangement tracks separated by role (melody/sub-melody/accompaniment)
- Musical system: Western diatonic/chromatic (chord annotations use Western notation)
- Language: Mandarin Chinese pop songs (lyrics not included, MIDI only)
- Modalities: MIDI (primary), beat annotations, chord annotations, key annotations
- Labels: beat positions, chord names with timestamps, key changes, arrangement role (melody/sub-melody/accompaniment)
