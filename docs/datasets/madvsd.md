## Source
- URL: https://github.com/CarlWangChina/MADVSD
- Paper: "Multi-Accent Mandarin Dry-Vocal Singing Dataset: Benchmark for Singing Accent Recognition"
- License: Not specified; PII concerns under legal review
- Access method: Currently unavailable for public download due to privacy concerns
- Status: gated

## Paper & Description Insights
MADVSD contains over 670 hours of dry vocal recordings from 4,206 native Mandarin speakers across nine distinct Chinese regions. Includes 12,618 complete a cappella performances. Each participant performs three popular songs in their native accent plus phonetic exercises covering all Mandarin vowels and full octave vocal ranges.

Nine regional accent types covered (abbreviated codes): SZR, JR, SSHR, BMLR, SGXR, FR, HHAR, GGR, YGSR — spanning China's geography.

Currently restricted due to non-redactable PII (names, hometowns) in recordings. Creators are undergoing legal review for controlled access frameworks. Sample audio from two speakers available for demonstration.

Extremely valuable for accent/dialect research in singing — largest dataset of its kind. The regional accent labels make it unique for studying regional variation in Chinese vocal performance.
