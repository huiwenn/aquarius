# China Sound and Music Technology Database (CSMTD)

## Source
- URL: https://ccmusic-database.github.io/en/database/csmtd.html
- Paper: Part of the CCMusic ecosystem (see `ccmusic.md`)
- License: Copyright held by Fudan University and Zhejiang University; "free and public" access stated
- Access method: GitHub, Zenodo, HuggingFace. Demo files on website.
- Status: umbrella (sub-datasets handled individually)
- Parent: CCMusic platform. Contains sub-datasets: GZ_IsoTech, GuZheng Midi-Wav, Chinese National Pentatonic Mode, PMEmo, Guzheng_Tech99, XFID. Each has its own doc.

## Paper & Description Insights
CSMTD is one of four main databases within the CCMusic platform. It focuses on sound and music technology research with emphasis on Guzheng performance, Chinese pentatonic modes, music emotion, and Xinjiang folk instruments. Unlike the core CCMusic paper datasets (audio-only), CSMTD includes MIDI files, pre-computed audio features, sheet music/MusicXML, physiological signals (EDA in PMEmo), and metadata annotations.

## Content & Taxonomy Analysis
- Time period: Contemporary recordings
- Region: Han Chinese (Guzheng, pentatonic modes), Xinjiang minorities (XFID: Dutar, Satar, Tanbur)
- Genre/form: Traditional instrumental solo, folk instrumental, pop music (PMEmo)
- Instrumentation: Guzheng (primary), Pipa, Gu Qin, Xinjiang folk instruments (Dutar, Satar, Tanbur)
- Musical system: Chinese pentatonic modes
- Modalities: Audio (WAV), MIDI, CSV features, sheet music/MusicXML, physiological signals, LRC lyrics, TXT annotations
- Notable: GuZheng Midi-Wav has ~300 audio files, ~20 hours, ~150,000 annotations; Guzheng_Tech99 has 99 compositions with 63,352 labels; PMEmo has 794 songs with emotion annotations
