## Paper & Description Insights
A symbolic music dataset for Guqin (古琴) music. The Guqin is one of the most ancient Chinese instruments — a seven-string plucked zither with over 3,000 years of history, closely associated with literati culture and Confucian philosophy.

Details about size, format, and annotations to be determined from repository inspection.

Guqin music uses a unique tablature notation system (jianzipu 减字谱) that differs from both Western notation and jianpu. A symbolic dataset capturing Guqin music fills an important gap, as Guqin repertoire is relatively well-documented historically but poorly represented in MIR datasets.
