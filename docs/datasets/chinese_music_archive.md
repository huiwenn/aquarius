## Paper & Description Insights
The Chinese Music Archive (中國民族音樂資料館) is a digital repository for preserving and accessing Chinese folk music materials. It functions as a browsing website rather than a downloadable dataset. Content includes audio/video recordings, historical photos, program notes, and informational resources on music history, instruments, composers, and performers.

Described as a "long-term and continuous Chinese folk music collection" indicating ongoing expansion. Trial version available; full access requires registration.

Coverage spans mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and diaspora communities worldwide.

## Content & Taxonomy Analysis
- Time period: Historical through contemporary
- Region: Pan-China (mainland, HK, Macau, Taiwan, diaspora)

- Instrumentation: Guqin, pipa, guzheng, erhu, dizi, percussion
- Musical system: Traditional Chinese
- Language: Chinese (Traditional/Simplified), English interface available
- Modalities: Audio/video recordings, historical photos, text documentation
- Labels: instrument, genre, region (based on browsing categories)
- Note: Not a structured dataset — web-based archive requiring manual browsing/scraping
