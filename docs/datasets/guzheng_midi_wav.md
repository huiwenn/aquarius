## Paper & Description Insights
A bi-directional MIDI-WAV database for Guzheng. Contains ~300 audio files totaling ~20 hours with ~150,000 annotations. Provides paired MIDI and WAV recordings, enabling research in MIDI-to-audio synthesis and audio-to-MIDI transcription for Guzheng.

This is one of the few datasets offering aligned MIDI-audio pairs for a traditional Chinese instrument, making it valuable for transcription and synthesis research.

## Content & Taxonomy Analysis
- Time period: Contemporary recordings of traditional and modern Guzheng repertoire
- Region: Han Chinese
- Genre/form: Guzheng solo performance
- Instrumentation: Guzheng (silk family)
- Musical system: Traditional Chinese
- Modalities: Audio (WAV), MIDI, annotations
- Labels: MIDI note events aligned with audio
- Notable: ~150,000 annotations across ~300 files — high annotation density
