## Paper & Description Insights
CODS is the first Cantonese opera vocal synthesis dataset. Derived from 29 original works by four famous performers with a total length of 3.81 hours. Addresses the shortage of high-quality opera datasets caused by poor audio quality and complex dialect annotation challenges.

Designed for singing synthesis technology and digital preservation of Cantonese opera. Audio-text aligned for vocal synthesis research.

Cantonese opera (粤剧, yueju) is a major regional opera tradition from Guangdong province, distinct from Beijing Opera (jingju) in language, melodic system, and performance practice.

- Language: Cantonese (粤语)
- Modalities: Audio (3.81 hours), text (lyrics/libretto), audio-text alignment

- Notable: First Cantonese opera dataset. Important for non-Mandarin Chinese opera research.
