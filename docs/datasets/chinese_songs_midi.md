## Paper & Description Insights
A collection of nearly 3,000 Chinese songs in MIDI format, obtained from an open-source music website. Intended for learning and reference purposes.

No academic paper associated. Unclear provenance (scraped from music website). Licensing is informal — "for learning purposes only." May have copyright concerns for redistribution.

Despite limitations, this is one of the largest collections of Chinese popular music in MIDI format, useful for symbolic music analysis of contemporary Chinese songs.
