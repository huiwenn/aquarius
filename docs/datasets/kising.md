## Paper & Description Insights
KiSing is described as "the first open-source Mandarin singing voice synthesis corpus." Originally released in 2021 by singer Kiki Zhang. ACE-KiSing extends the original with eight additional songs (including four English songs, making it bilingual) and corresponding music scores.

Part of a broader ecosystem of Mandarin singing corpora alongside Opencpop and M4Singer. ACE-KiSing available via ESPnet-Muskits.
