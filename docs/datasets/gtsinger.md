## Paper & Description Insights
GTSinger is a large global, multi-technique, high-quality singing corpus with realistic music scores, designed for all singing tasks. Accepted as NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight. Size ~54.2 GB.

Covers five languages: Chinese, English, Spanish, German, Russian — making it multilingual (not Chinese-only). Five singing technique groups annotated: Control Group, Falsetto, Mixed Voice, Paired Speech, Pharyngeal. Audio segments range 2.4–14.2 seconds.

Notable for multi-language and multi-technique coverage — bridges Chinese singing with global context.
